Rapid review

A fixed-price first look at a software project or a software business. Two days with your systems and your people, and written findings within a week.

When to use it

  • You are about to invest in or acquire a software business, and want to know what you would be buying.
  • You sponsor a project where the slides say one thing and the delivery data says another.
  • You have had software built by an agency or with coding agents, and want an independent read before you rely on it.

What we look at

  • How the software is built: structure, tests, dependencies and the security basics.
  • How it ships: the pipeline, how often it releases, and what happens when a release fails.
  • How the work is run: what was planned against what was delivered.
  • Who knows what, and what happens if they leave.

What you get

  • A written report: findings, the evidence for each, a risk rating and what to do next.
  • A readout with whoever needs to hear it.
  • A straight answer on whether a deeper review is warranted.

What we need from you

Before anything is shared: a signed NDA and engagement letter.

Scope

A first look finds the red flags. It is not a full audit, a penetration test or a line-by-line code review, and the report says so. If it finds smoke, a full review is quoted separately, from $20,000 plus GST.

Price

$8,000 plus GST, fixed.

NSW Government agencies: available through the ICT Services Scheme (SCM0020), scoped and quoted under the scheme's terms.

Independence

We don't do delivery work for organisations we review. Nothing in the report is there to sell you the fix.
